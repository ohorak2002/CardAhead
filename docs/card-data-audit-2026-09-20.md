## What changed in calculations and provenance

- Exact merchant aliases replace broad Costco warehouse eligibility. Restricted
  portal/direct-booking benefits remain conditional without confirmation. Cash
  versus points is explicit; graphical comparisons stay within reward currency.
- Map types and merchant identity are not issuer MCCs. Recommendations explain
  uncertainty; issuer-specific caveats retain priority in notification text.
  Physical geofence planning excludes online-only categories. Merchant-name offer
  matching uses exact case/diacritic-normalized aliases, never substring/fuzzy matching.
- Cap-crossing purchases split bonus and base earning using Decimal money. Frozen
  recommendation cap remaining/base values protect historical estimates from
  later wallet edits; old snapshots retain their original numbers. The local and
  cloud views distinguish unknown comparison baselines from zero additional value.
- New catalog cards carry an accepted baseline. Personal changes/disables are
  tracked per benefit; editing does not strip the product ID. Reviewing a catalog
  update preserves adjustments. Old wallets are preserved and marked for review,
  rather than retroactively claiming their saved rates were freshly verified.
- Personal offers check dates, enrollment, exact scope, channel, minimum spend,
  remaining uses, reward and spend caps. Unknown purchase/eligibility/stacking stays
  conditional. Only the best single personal offer is counted; no unproven
  offer-to-offer stacking. Credits are capped at purchase value and remaining
  user-reported reward allowance. Recorded use does not enroll with an issuer.

## Limits

There is no issuer transaction feed or independent merchant-code/received-reward
verification. Usage is volunteered; unreported usage can overstate remaining
eligibility. Calendar resets use UTC; unusual anniversary/half-year/multi-purchase
threshold terms must be entered as dated offers and reviewed against the issuer.
Insurance exclusions and account migration rules are not fully machine-modelled.
Unsupported benefits are labeled or described, not guessed into reward rates.
An issuer may change terms after this audit. Inaccessible pages should be rechecked
before publishing a new “verified” date.
